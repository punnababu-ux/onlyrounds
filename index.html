Delivery modes

Two delivery modes. The same rigorous round, either way.

INBOUND

Candidate-initiated

One URL on job boards, WhatsApp or a QR. Candidates start the round themselves, in the browser.

  • Link, QR or WhatsApp
  • No app, no scheduling
  • Runs outside office hours
OUTBOUND

AI-initiated

Upload a list. The agent calls, runs the same round, handles retries and files the report.

  • Bulk list upload
  • Automatic retries
  • Any volume, same day
